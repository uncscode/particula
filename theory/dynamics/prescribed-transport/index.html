      Transport in prescribed wind fields

      How much of the width of a tracer puff comes from atmospheric mixing, and how much from its numerical representation? A prescribed wind supplies a mean velocity and a thermodynamic history. A supplied eddy diffusivity describes the unresolved spread about that mean. Molecular diffusion describes thermal motion at the molecular scale. Both coefficients have units of m²/s, but the eddy diffusivity depends on the flow, observation scale and averaging time. A point release in uniform wind provides a control for measuring spread before interpreting a terrain or CFD calculation. Compare the same puff on grids and as particles.
