      Dimensional collision kernel

      Section titled “Dimensional collision kernel”

      For an ion and an aerosol particle, let be their summed radii (m), their reduced mass (kg) and their reduced friction coefficient (kg/s). The attraction or repulsion energy relative to thermal energy determines the dimensionless kinetic and continuum enhancement factors and . Particula converts a dimensionless collision kernel to a pair coefficient in m³/s using

      The choice of specifies the transition between limiting regimes and the range of electrostatic interactions that the fit represents. Use this pair coefficient with the relevant number concentrations to estimate encounter rates. The pair calculation alone does not evolve a charge distribution.
