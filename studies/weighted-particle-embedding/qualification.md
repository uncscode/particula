# Qualification of forecasts

These criteria apply to the reserved final scenarios. They were specified after inspecting pilot and validation runs. The test and challenge trajectories are generated independently and remain outside fitting, preprocessing, checkpoint selection, and model design.

For each observable and time, estimate the deterministic reference uncertainty as the sum of the two members' absolute changes at the last timestep halving. Resolve a mixing-state difference only where its absolute value exceeds four times this estimate. Report the fraction of resolvable families alongside sign accuracy. For a forecast-horizon claim, require at least 90% correct signs on those families and mean absolute paired error at most 25% of their mean absolute resolved signal. Score wetting and drying separately. Also report individual-member absolute errors, so cancellation of a shared error is visible.

For the closed pulse, dry material and number must remain unchanged. Enforce equal-and-opposite gas and particle transfer within each leg, exact prescribed resets, finite values and nonnegative inventories. Check number loss for Brownian coagulation, material conservation after every observation, and convergence of independently seeded ensemble statistics. The learned deterministic mean cannot recover the random collision realization of a native checkpoint. Report both its error against individual realizations and the uncertainty of the ensemble mean.

Evaluate from a single initial state and from 4 or 16 observed frames when available, with exactly the same future forcing and horizon. For the longest qualified contiguous horizon, require all earlier resolved observations to meet the criteria. Report isolated passing later observations separately. Keep native, decoded-true-state, and free-running diagnostics alongside model, sectional and persistence results.

## Conditional composition tails

Reserve 64 new families each for an interpolation test and a forcing challenge. Both vary the total probability assigned to the two composition tails uniformly between 0.05 and 0.90. The composition-tail generator uses family IDs 7,600,000–7,600,063 for the test and 8,600,000–8,600,063 for the challenge. The challenge draws vapor levels and dry population parameters from the original challenge generator. Generate native references at 32, 64 and 128 substeps for each of two matched aerosols. Keep predictions and scores on both cohorts sealed until the model manifest is frozen. Apply the original positive-lead resolution, sign, relative-contrast and conservation rules to both.

## Two volatile materials

Reserve test families 12,000,000–12,000,063 and challenge families 13,000,000–13,000,063. Each family supplies both particle mixtures and both phase schedules. On the challenge, increase wet-pulse water activity, composition contrast and the semivolatile pulse amplitude using the generator's disjoint ranges. Assess water and the synthetic semivolatile individually on the 64-to-128-step uncertainty bound. Report the rarest resolved material and schedule alongside pooled results, and verify closed reservoirs for both species.

## Independently timed vapor pulses

Reserve 40 paired families in each of the ranges starting at 16,000,000 (test) and 17,000,000 (challenge). Each runs twelve plateaus with 144 advancing intervals of 2.5 ms. A prescribed reset can change water alone, semivolatile alone, or both. Carry the freely evolving gas and both particle inventories over each reset. Compare proportional initial organic loading with a size-matched allocation correlated with dry composition. The correlation sign alternates by family. Replay each compressed state with both a proportional decoder and a decoder that retains two extra organic moments per size node.

Compare against matched 64- and 128-step native trajectories with the existing material-specific resolution and positive-lead criteria. Report paired differences, individual errors, the pooled horizon, and the weakest of six preassigned strata defined by family ID modulo six (reset order and organic correlation sign). Treat the stratum results as small-sample estimates and give resolved counts. Both members and all observations of a family remain together in uncertainty estimates.

## Volatile co-location

The binary loading panel reserves 48 paired families starting at IDs 20,000,000 and 21,000,000 for test and challenge. Within each dry size/composition class, each volatile has the same high/low marginal measure in the two members. Compare uniform correlation, correlation that reverses by dry size, and correlation that cancels within each size. Use 64- and 128-step native references for each vapor. Test the marginal record, one global cross term, size-conditioned cross terms, and size/composition-conditioned cross terms on the same native states.

The ternary panel reserves 36 paired families starting at IDs 24,000,000 and 25,000,000. Each has three water levels and three organic levels. The two members match both marginal measures and their covariance exactly in every dry class. The cubic construction first differs in a water-squared/organic mixed moment. The quartic construction first differs in the water-squared/organic-squared moment. Compare the native 128- and 256-step references for both uniform and composition-dependent allocations. Compare explicit moments through degree four against learned particle features of widths one through four, trained on a separate bank of positive joint distributions. Apply the same per-vapor sign, amplitude and contiguous-horizon criteria and report how many differences exceed the numerical uncertainty.

For the hybrid reset comparison, use the recurrent four-expert model fitted on 1,024 families from the mixed curriculum, seed 3201. Compare zero, two, four and eight protected frames after each prescribed gas reset, including the initial state. Each protected frame uses four physical substeps of the third-order, three-iteration solver. Hold the recurrent memory during a protected frame. The reset time is known at forecast initialization. Report the number of expensive advances, the weakest of the 24 species/loading/reset strata, and both forecast errors at equal output cadence.

## Transfer to another volatile material

Reserve 48 paired families each beginning at 28,000,000 (test) and 29,000,000 (challenge). Evaluate two, three and four simultaneously exchanging volatile species on the same dry populations, with four partial or full gas changes over 100 ms. Challenge families have stronger subsequent gas supplies. Train each shared particle head on two species, three species or alternating two/three species. The fourth material is unseen by all fits. Freeze the feed-forward, recurrent, species-attention and mixture checkpoints at 1,800 updates, seed 4001. Cross these with one and four substeps on the same ten decoded nodes. Include the uncorrected and third-order physical solvers. Score each volatile against native 64/128-step references by the same paired signal and conservation criteria. Report individual species errors and the weakest lane at each species count.

Compare the same four guard lengths for the feed-forward and GRU checkpoints from seed 3201 on the identical two-vapor cohort. In validation, repeat four-frame protection for all three training seeds. Compare the twelve-plateau mixture under coarser output cadences, including a 10 ms guard with a 2.5 ms internal integration limit. Report which portion of any apparent gain depends on the chosen observations.

## Observable hydration history

Reserve 96 paired families each beginning at 32,000,000 (test) and 33,000,000 (challenge). Both members have the same dry nodes, bulk water and organic mass, and previous and prescribed gas concentrations at the forecast origin. The assignments of those two volatile inventories across the dry sizes differ. Supply either the present record or two to eight preceding bulk observations ending at that record. Eight frames span 35 µs. The subsequent wet/dry forcing and solvers are identical. The challenge uses smaller radii and stronger departures from uniform loading. No family IDs, member labels or future concentrations enter a learned predictor.

Freeze the seed-4201 present-only, feed-forward, GRU and expert-mixture decoders trained on 8,192 separate families. Compare two and eight observations, a physical inverse fit, uniform initial loading, and directly supplied allocation. Evaluate noiseless histories and 0.01 mg m⁻³ independent Gaussian noise per earlier observation while preserving the current inventories. Test the wrong-history control by exchanging past observations between the pair. Report the allocation error and free-running volatile contrasts through 80 ms against the native 64-to-128-step uncertainty. Independent forward BDF replay validates the short history construction on the validation cohort.

For validation, estimate the distribution of hidden water and organic allocations consistent with noisy observed histories. Construct an equally spaced two-dimensional grid, evaluate the physical history at each candidate and fit the allocation prior using only training coefficients. Apply the observation likelihood separately to each aerosol. Check whether posterior averaging and interval widths explain forecast failures as the organic signal approaches the measurement noise. Vary grid resolution on the same families and preserve the physical inverse as a continuous-fit control.

## Longer and more closely matched aerosol histories

Carry the two-vapor initial populations, finite reservoirs and predeclared size-dependent organic allocation from twelve to 96 plateaus (2.88 s). For the longer independently forced benchmark, use the first 24 paired test and challenge families from the twelve-plateau panel. Verify both native refinements on their entire common prefix. Evaluate the same seed-3201 instantaneous, recurrent and mixed heads with zero, two and four protected observations. Use the fully physical solver throughout as a control. Report 12-, 24-, 48- and 96-plateau prefixes, all six reset strata and each volatile separately.

For the additional hydration experiment reserve 96 paired families each beginning at 36,000,000 (test) and 37,000,000 (challenge). Adjust the water and organic gradients to make the liquid volumes cancel at each dry size. The pair now has the same dry sizes, wet sizes, number and bulk composition at the forecast origin. Use the same fixed models from the original 8,192-family training, observed pasts, noise levels and native 64/128-step references as the hydration-history panel. A separate validation comparison adds four size-specific current diameters and varies their relative precision. Compare the consequent inference with prior knowledge of the two individual volatile allocations.

Also compare four shared, recursive allocation estimators trained on 65,536 separate families. A cubic approximation fitted from 16 physically generated pasts is specific to the dry state. Freeze three updates per inference, the physical update and its feed-forward, GRU and mixed corrections at seed 4401. All learned variants use 6,000 training updates. Rebuild the observation surface from each input before comparing the same two noise levels. Report its construction cost separately from inference on the fitted surface.
