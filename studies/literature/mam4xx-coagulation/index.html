      Studies / Literature analyses

      How many particles stay below 100 nm after a day of coagulation in polluted air? Starting from the same fine-mode sizes, the unmodified MAM4xx coagulation calculation leaves 3,379 particles cm⁻³. It models Brownian collisions in the fine modes. Our extension adds coarse-particle collisions and neutral van der Waals attraction to the modal calculation and leaves 2,424 cm⁻³. A pilot with the broad polluted spectrum exhausted the initial limit of 128 internal steps. The polluted runs allow up to 4,096 internal steps. The GPU schedules guarded work up to the cap, which makes an unnecessarily high cap costly even in dilute air.
