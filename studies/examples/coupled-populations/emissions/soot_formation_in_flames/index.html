      Follow organic uptake onto seeds during imposed cooling

      Section titled “Follow organic uptake onto seeds during imposed cooling”

      How can cooling alter gas–particle partitioning while collisions change particle size? This example follows 24 organic species around a supplied seed population, applying condensation and Brownian coagulation under a prescribed temperature history. It connects volatility assumptions to mass and size diagnostics.

      Despite the historical filename, this is not a soot-formation chemistry model. There is no nucleation, reaction network, carbonization, oxidation, or aggregate morphology. Fullerene-labeled seeds already exist at the start. Material tables and cooling are illustrative, not a calibrated flame or plume. Read organic partitioning and resolved coagulation for the separate mechanisms.

      Define the species and audit the property lookup

      Section titled “Define the species and audit the property lookup”

      The list spans small aromatics, PAHs, and a fullerene seed label. Supplied gas mass fractions are normalized to one; they are not tied here to a measured emission profile. The utility resolves names before retrieving properties. A returned numerical value does not establish a correct identity or validity at flame temperatures; inspect the resolved identifiers and property methods before using this as a material model.

      Build temperature-dependent tables and expose their limits

      Section titled “Build temperature-dependent tables and expose their limits”

      There are 5000 sampled particles at 10⁷ cm⁻³, represented in 5×10⁻¹⁰ m³. The total initial organic gas concentration is 5×10⁻⁵ kg/m³. These chosen conditions are not qualified as a particular combustion source.

      Vapor-pressure and surface-tension tables use 200 points from 200–1500 K. Surface tensions replace NaNs and impose a 0.02 N/m floor. Those numerical substitutions are model assumptions, not measurements at high temperature. The separately plotted vapor-pressure array is clipped after the strategies are constructed; that display operation should not be read as proof of the strategies’ underlying property validity. The charts help locate strong trends and questionable extrapolations; they do not establish chemical stability.

      Supply the condensation sites rather than create them

      Section titled “Supply the condensation sites rather than create them”

      Two lognormal seed-radius modes at 1 and 5 nm, both with geometric standard deviation 1.8, populate the resolved sample. All initial particle mass occupies the final, fullerene-labeled lane. The initial histogram counts particles per log-radius bin; it does not show a nucleation rate.

      Ideal molar activity and a mass-weighted surface treatment are imposed. The seed has a separate phase label from the other organics. At the smallest sizes, continuum surface and transport assumptions themselves need scrutiny. The species-inventory helper includes gas mass times represented volume, so later closed material comparisons use kg per species.

      Apply mass transfer and collisions as separate operations

      Section titled “Apply mass transfer and collisions as separate operations”

      Condensation uses a common diffusivity 2×10⁻⁵ m²/s and accommodation one. The fullerene lane (index 23) is excluded from partitioning, so its gas and particle inventories do not exchange through that process. Brownian coagulation can merge the existing resolved particles. Neither operation creates new chemical species or new seed particles.
