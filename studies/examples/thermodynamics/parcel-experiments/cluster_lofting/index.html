      Section titled “Where does a particle group travel in wind and updraft?”

      Follow 1,024 individual particles through a vertical stack, a periodic row, and a block of cells moving with the wind. Each layout contains four cells in one connected domain. Group A and group B each contain two adjacent cells. Each particle has its own ID, position and three component masses. The same IDs carry the same radii and recipes through all three experiments.

      The prescribed air velocity is 0.70 m/s east and 0.022 m/s up. There is no buoyancy feedback. The moving block translates rigidly with the air. Radii and composition stay fixed in uniform air at 293.15 K and 101325 Pa. This CPU example asks which particles change groups and how much of each species they carry through a face.

      Python · section 1

      Release the same population into three layouts

      Section titled “Release the same population into three layouts”

      Set a particle’s species masses from its spherical volume: component mass is total volume times that component’s volume fraction and material density. The sum of component masses divided by total volume gives the density used for settling. Each radius includes pure ammonium sulfate, sodium chloride, sucrose and an internal mixture of all three. These are specified compact dry particles. The ternary volume fractions are 40% ammonium sulfate, 35% sodium chloride and 25% sucrose. Densities of 1770 and 1587 kg/m³ follow the chamber example for ammonium sulfate and sucrose. We assign sodium chloride 2165 kg/m³, close to the reported solid density of 2.16 g/cm³. Water uptake, shape changes and chemistry are held fixed.

      The stack’s four layers are 7 cm deep. A contains the bottom pair. The A–B interface is at 14 cm and the open top is at 28 cm. The periodic row has four half-meter cells, A to the west and B to the east. The moving block has two columns, with A below B. In each cell, every size–recipe pair is released at two east/height locations with eight individual particles per location. A seeded stratified draw gives each particle a different north coordinate. The four radii and recipes are equally abundant. With uniform air and no collisions, particles released together in east and height have matching trajectories if their size and density also match.

      replicas_per_stratum controls population size and seed controls the transverse release coordinates. The default run advances 4 × 4 × 4 × 2 × 8 particle records. Each record counts as one physical particle.

      Compare transport against constant velocity and close each species budget

      Section titled “Compare transport against constant velocity and close each species budget”

      A particle of terminal speed travels east at the wind speed and rises at . Subtract mesh displacement to express its movement relative to the local cells. The kinematic reference and the mover use the same Stokes–Cunningham terminal-speed law and air viscosity. Check that each particle Reynolds number remains below the mover’s creeping- flow limit of 0.1. Check every physical ID at each observation against its expected world and mesh displacement within 1 nm. A periodic wrap adds one domain length to eastward travel. After a particle exits the fixed stack, keep its last displacement in the ensemble mean and its mass in outflow.

      Use two independent budgets. Sum the active records in A and B and the records at the open boundary. Separately accumulate the signed transfers from the initial group inventories. Both methods must give the same particle count and mass of each species in each group at every sample.

      In the periodic row all 1,024 particles remain in the domain after four or five wraps. The cumulative sodium chloride mass passing from A to B is 4.752 µg, matched by 4.752 µg passing from B back to A. In the fixed stack 704 particles exit through the top, carrying 15.3% of the initial ammonium sulfate mass, 13.7% of sodium chloride and 23.3% of sucrose.
