      Which parcel solver reaches the same accuracy targets faster?

      Section titled “Which parcel solver reaches the same accuracy targets faster?”

      In a rising parcel, small wet particles can return to equilibrium much faster than the parcel cools. Compare two dry size spectra under the same ascent. Explicit RK23 resolves the fastest droplet relaxation directly. Linearly implicit ROS3 solves for the coupled stage increments. Which takes less elapsed time on one GPU when both must reproduce the cloud response and its sensitivity to updraft?

      Hold the ascent and observation times fixed

      Section titled “Hold the ascent and observation times fixed”

      Begin with the 45, 70 and 110 nm dry radii from the fixed-step parcel. For the wider spectrum, move the outer classes to 30 and 150 nm. Both have the same counts and hygroscopicity. Changing both tails changes dry volume as well as the spread in sizes. In this closed parcel, 1 kg of dry air rises for 100 s at 1 m/s. Only the observation cadence is fixed. Each CUDA solver chooses its own internal steps. The controller tests the embedded pressure, class liquid volume, vapor and temperature errors against the same relative tolerance. The scale floors are 10⁻⁸ for normalized pressure and liquid/dry volume ratio, and 10⁻¹² kg/kg dry air for vapor. For RK23, a stability check also caps the step duration times the estimated relaxation rate at two. Both methods use the default minimum retry step of 10⁻⁹ s and limit of 100,000 total trials. SciPy’s implicit Radau solver supplies an independent CPU trajectory sampled at the same times. A CUDA-capable GPU is required to run this example.

      Diagnose activation, water and pressure work

      Section titled “Diagnose activation, water and pressure work”

      A class counts as activated when its wet radius passes the maximum of the full κ-Köhler equilibrium curve at the current temperature. With wet-to-dry radius ratio , let and let be the Kelvin length divided by dry radius. The critical point satisfies . On the large-radius side the right side is larger. Weight classes by represented number to obtain the fraction above critical radius at each observation. Kelvin correction

      Reconstruct liquid water from the class radii to check it against vapor loss. At initial equilibrium the restoring rate for a small radius perturbation at fixed ambient conditions is proportional to the slope of the κ-Köhler curve divided by the wet radius. The vapor-diffusion and heat resistance is common to the two spectra at the shared initial temperature and pressure, so the ratio of these slopes gives a relative relaxation rate. Compare the parcel’s sensible and latent energy changes with the pressure work integrated from its volume and pressure at the saved times. The same trapezoidal quadrature is applied to all three integrators.

      Perturb the updraft and let the controller choose steps again

      Section titled “Perturb the updraft and let the controller choose steps again”

      For each tolerance, differentiate terminal liquid water at 100 s along the baseline accepted CUDA steps. Check it by running the ascent at 0.999 and 1.001 m/s.
