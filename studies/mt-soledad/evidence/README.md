# Numerical comparisons used in the Mt. Soledad study

Each row is a saved model result or an aggregate of the field observations. Ratios compare the denuded path with bypass. Differences in diameter and concentration use denuded minus bypass. The original field study and instrument diagram describe the two paths and their shared upstream particle reference. [Ravichandran et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adx0960) · [CCN archive](https://doi.org/10.6075/J0KH0NHG)

| Comparison | Files and units |
| --- | --- |
| Integrated activation at 0.6% supersaturation | [Full size range](integrated-counts.csv) and [120–250 nm by date](large-seed-date-means.csv), concentrations and branch differences in CCN/cm³. The saved whole-window series uses UTC days since 1970-01-01, with one row per held date. Inspection of the April archive revealed some reconstructed CCN concentrations over 100 times the coincident total-particle concentrations. The calendar exclusion followed inspection. Figure S5 also documents an April instrument malfunction. [Supplement](https://www.science.org/doi/suppl/10.1126/sciadv.adx0960/suppl_file/sciadv.adx0960_sm.pdf) |
| Optical response and transport | [Training parameters](optical-training.csv), [held models and floor sensitivity](optical-controls.csv), [21-date processed counter comparison](processed-counter.csv). Delay is in seconds, wet diameter and optical floor in µm. Growth delays change size before counting. The scalar alternative multiplies number at every size by the same fraction. The interval columns give the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of resampled dates. [Plotted channel values](../figures/optical-spectrum.csv) |
| Submicron chemistry and water uptake | [Held 60 nm growth](growth-scores.csv), [paired differences](growth-contrasts.csv), [three small-filter dates](small-filter-growth.csv), [salt standards](salt-standards.csv) and [near-dry class](near-dry-class.csv). Growth factor is wet diameter divided by dry diameter. The AS and NaCl columns in the primary scores apply the apparent humidities implied by the respective spherical salt controls. A positive paired error contrast means the fully interacting model increased absolute error over the separate-pool model. [Growth measurements](https://doi.org/10.6075/J08G8KWG) |
| Transfer of the recipes to cloud activation | [Bypass activation](activation.csv). The dry threshold, apparent midpoint, 25–75% activation width and corresponding absolute errors are in nm. These rows apply the diameter and spread mapping calibrated at 0.4%. |
| Finite interfacial organic material | [Upper organic mass fractions](c16-bounds.csv) and [June 10 upper-bound reservoir](june-film.csv). The 0.5×, 1× and 2× conditions scale growth exposure. Wet diameters are in µm. These scenarios reuse independent held outlet conditions. The field-filter and raw optical selections had no simultaneous qualifying windows. |

The [selector](select_tables.py) records each table’s upstream filename, filtering rules and averaging. To regenerate after rebuilding the archived study stages, run `python select_tables.py PATH_TO_STUDY_STAGES` in this directory. The four manuscript plot tables and their renderer are in [figures](../figures/). See the [science guide](../README.md) for the field archives and calculation source.
