# Can one parameter vector predict the Jia trays across wind and retrieval time?

Fit the complete archive with one wind-independent vector per model. The blind model starts at release using a common lognormal airborne mass spectrum. The conditional model sees each wind's measured 30-minute tray mass and spectrum as inputs and forecasts later trays. Its wind holdout tests the original early-to-late transfer question. In the blind wind holdout all tray measurements from that wind are excluded. Both models see wind and the recorded bulk air concentration through prediction time.

Use the same size-dependent sticking threshold and Particula fall speeds as the rebound study. Smooth grain-to-grain variation in threshold to permit differentiation. Allow wind-driven normal impact dispersion, progressively stronger sticking after 30 minutes, lagged transport of the monitor concentration, size-selective airborne depletion and removal around the observed 6 m/s mobilization threshold. Supply and retention share parameters across all winds. Test the extra terms by refitting nested models. Do not tune wind-specific constants.

Fit all six cumulative threshold masses at five retrievals and both full 41-channel spectra for the blind case. For the conditional case the early observation defines the initial state. Use endpoint replicate means and intermediate kinetic-sheet means. Weight mass residuals by reported group SEM with a 0.75 mg floor. Use a 0.10 Hellinger distance as a declared shape scale. Average the squared standardized mass and shape residuals separately. Scores have no statistical likelihood interpretation because thresholds overlap and replicate spectra are unavailable. Evaluate absolute mass error, D50 error, full-spectrum total variation and 10–20 µm mass separately.

Run bounded, seeded multistart optimization with automatic gradients. Refit every leave-one-wind-out split, each late retrieval time separately, and a forward holdout after 1.5 h. Compare with persistence and an early-conditioned common power law. Probe model terms, modality weights, alternative data conventions, external impact scales and local parameter sensitivities. These are retrospective blocked comparisons within one previously inspected experiment. Success requires useful withheld predictions for size and mass across winds.

The first wind refits exposed a tradeoff. The common growth law gave smaller total-mass errors, and the fitted surface model gave smaller spectrum errors. As an exploratory test, normalize the surface model's channel predictions to the common growth law and jointly refit both parts. Also test a reduced version with constant adhesion, prompt delivery and zero airborne sorting. Freeze this comparison before the additional corner tests. For each corner, fit only the other three winds through 1.5 h, then score the omitted wind at 2 and 3 h. The omitted wind's early tray remains an input to all conditioned variants. This is a stricter repartition of the same previously inspected archive.
