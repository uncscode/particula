# Fixed comparison for the follow-up

Start from the same 30-minute observation for each paired run. Test whether wind-dependent exchange among equal-volume vertical air parcels, a competing sidewall sink, and post-30-minute sticking collisions move the three-hour retained dry-volume median toward the measured value. At each wind the control uses settling and interparcel exchange. Switch on sidewall loss alone, then Brownian, turbulent shear, and differential-settling collisions separately and together. Compare 0, 50 and 85% RH (assumed κ=0.05) and neutral, like-sign and symmetric bipolar charges. Keep the early deposit fixed within each wind, including when changing later wind forcing. Check both the observed 2 m/s median and the 10–20 µm volume share. All models share the initial height and size-inventory reconstruction and absorbing floor. No endpoint is used to select kernel, charge, uptake or sticking parameters. Treat the published balance resolution, laser QA and missing replicate data separately from numerical error and model spread.
