# Can 30 minutes predict the three-hour tray deposit?

Study requested 24 November 2026. Repeat Jia et al. Figure 4, DOI 10.5194/acp-26-14245-2026, from Zenodo record 22962214 v2. Use the authors’ normalized laser-diffraction volume channels and log-interpolated cumulative D50. Compare published one-decimal medians to the archive at a 0.1 µm tolerance. The paper reports the later observations, so this is an untuned retrospective test.

Run 0 m/s first. Freeze the operator for 2, 4 and 6 m/s. For each wind the only spectrum input is the cumulative 0.5 h tray deposit. The reference copies this volume distribution. The settling case infers a relative initial airborne inventory by dividing each early channel by its modeled collected fraction. Advance to 3 h using a mixed column with an absorbing lower boundary, no ongoing source, no change in particle size, no other losses and size-independent conversion from model floor to tray. Use H = 2 m, spherical particles of density 2650 kg/m³, illustrative dry air at 293.15 K and 101325 Pa, Particula slip and drag. The fan setting selects the early input. It does not alter the operator.

Score three-hour D50 and signed error in µm, modal channel, total variation (half the sum of absolute channel-share differences), and change in ≤10, 10–20, 20–30 and >30 µm volume shares. Assign each reported channel to a band by its labeled diameter. Success requires improvement over unchanged in D50 and channel distance, with modes and bands as additional checks. Distinguish sign from accuracy. Repeat at H=0.5, 1, 2, 4 m and density=1500, 2000, 2650, 3000 kg/m³; the upper heights stress the effective residence time. Challenge well-mixed behavior with a uniformly seeded, unmixed, absorbing column and check log-channel quadrature convergence.

Compare observed retained mass per tray (means of the archived five trays per endpoint) to the mass growth implied by the same channel factors, using the early <63 µm mean and size-independent density. Diagnose 10–20 µm increments from the authors’ cumulative threshold masses. Separate composition from absolute changes. Time groups are distinct trays, and precision at the channel level is unavailable. Avoid a causal claim about shear, aggregation or re-entrainment. Study ends when the frozen predictions, checks and a competing physical assumption bound what an early spectrum alone can identify.
