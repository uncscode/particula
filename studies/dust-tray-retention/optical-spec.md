# Optical tests for the reported tray size distributions

Treat the eight published volume spectra as processed data. Test the sign and size of the early-to-late median shift, the exact-cutoff 10–20 µm fraction and the three-hour fraction below 20 µm. Carry candidate spectra through the conditional global model with its wind-holdout parameters frozen. In particular compare to the 2 m/s observed coarsening and the 6 m/s archive discrepancy. Preserve the distinction between measured tray mass and size-specific mass inferred from optical fractions.

Test an angular-scattering instrument as described by the paper and a scanning obscuration instrument as described by the manufacturer. For diffraction, synthesize signals with Particula sphere scattering at 520 nm in water. Recover candidate size distributions under alternative dust refractive indices, angular coverage, smoothness and imposed detector perturbations. Evaluate inversion residuals, matched-optics recovery and a constrained range that does not require choosing a particular smooth solution. For transit sizing, investigate accepted central chords of randomly oriented and aligned constant-volume spheroids and partial rejection of off-centre chords. Keep the same response for the two endpoints in the primary test. Challenge that assumption separately. Scenario ranges and synthetic signal tolerances describe assumed systems. Actual instrument uncertainty requires the recorded event or angle data, replicate distributions and processing settings.
