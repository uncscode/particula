# What gas and volatility evidence can distinguish in Manchester

The campaign fit uses precursor disappearance as a measured input and scores suspended organic mass and total particle number. The modeled reservoirs describe precursor-tagged effective product mass. Product-specific mass spectra and volatility measurements provide an external comparison. The following comparisons with independent measurements are qualitative because the pools carry neither elemental formulae nor calibrated saturation concentrations.

| Candidate | Observed chemical or physical analogue | Remaining ambiguity |
|---|---|---|
| α-pinene-derived vapor returns to labile particles, V_A → P_A | The ternary thermal-denuder retrieval placed about 70% of the aerosol mass at effective saturation concentrations of 1–100 µg m⁻³. FIGAERO-CIMS assigned about 80% of the particle signal to 10–100 µg m⁻³. Both show that appreciable material can partition over the chamber loading range. [Voliotis et al., Fig. 3 and §3.2](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/22/13677/2022/) | These techniques weight compounds differently. They leave the identity of V_A and the area-normalized one-way return rate unresolved. |
| α-pinene vapor self-accretion or mixed α-pinene/o-cresol accretion | Direct measurements during α-pinene ozonolysis linked C16–C20 gas dimers to peroxy radical cross-reactions. At Manchester, mixed and single-precursor systems shared abundant particle products, alongside some mixture-specific compounds. [Zhao et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30413618/) · [Shao et al., Figs. 6–7](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/22/9799/2022/) | A precursor-tagged gas reservoir can contain many molecules. The Manchester formulae leave the reactant identities, carbon origins and immediate transfer of a dimer to particles unresolved. |
| Evaporation of labile o-cresol-derived particles, P_C → V_C | The fraction of FIGAERO particle signal above effective C* = 10 µg m⁻³ was 17% at full o-cresol reactivity and 40% at half reactivity. [Voliotis et al., §3.1](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/22/13677/2022/) | This dependence on chamber loading is qualitatively compatible with a volatile particle fraction. Those signal fractions cannot calibrate a shared evaporation rate or the mass assigned to P_C. |
| Loss of the o-cresol-derived vapor reservoir to terminal products, V_C → F | Laboratory wavelength-dependent photolysis of butenedial establishes a route from a ring-cleavage dicarbonyl to smaller products. [Butenedial gas-phase photolysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0009261405006779) | The chamber fit folds illumination, precursor speciation and branching into an effective first-order coefficient. A decline in V_C can also represent wall loss or conversion to an unsampled gas. |
| Return of secondary vapor from the o-cresol product family to retained particle material, U_CC → L_CC | In the pure o-cresol experiments, products assigned C₇H₇NO₄ accounted for 48% of the FIGAERO particle signal at full initial reactivity and 34% at half reactivity. [Voliotis et al., §3.1](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/22/13677/2022/) | The o-cresol-derived particle material and its loading dependence support a qualitative partitioning comparison. The formula does not establish that the measured species came from a dimer-derived vapor, or that its uptake follows this area-scaled rate. |
| Further oxidation and fragmentation within particle-derived product families | Aqueous-phase OH aging experiments on α-pinene SOA tracked the loss of dimeric material and formation of oxidized products. [Aqueous aging of α-pinene SOA](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.est.2c07630) | The phase, oxidant exposure, fragment retention and subsequent vapor uptake of each Manchester pool remain assumptions. Identical suspended masses can accompany different internal product transfers. |

A discriminating follow-up measurement would combine precursor-specific isotopic labeling with time-resolved gas and particle spectra in the same mixing experiment. A mixed dimer should carry carbon from both labeled precursor families. Changing seed surface area at fixed precursor dose could independently test an inferred vapor-return rate. Those tests would link a predicted transfer to an observation beyond the fitted mass and count histories.
