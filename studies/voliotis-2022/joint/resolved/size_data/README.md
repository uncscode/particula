# Manchester number-size observations

## Run 24 distribution

Wang et al. (2022), supplement Figure S1(a), plot a number-size distribution against time for the ternary experiment labeled “0730.” Voliotis Table S1 and the July 30 instrument EDFs identify this as run 24. The upper panel is a number distribution. Its red and magenta markers are measurement diameters. Its solid black line is geometric mean diameter and its dashed envelope is ±50% of that mean. The lower panel is a size-resolved SOA mass fraction on a *vacuum aerodynamic* diameter axis, a different observable. [Wang et al., Fig. S1 and caption, p. 5](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/22/4149/2022/acp-22-4149-2022-supplement.pdf#page=5) · [Run 24 instrument record](https://doi.org/10.25326/1D6D-TQ82)

The two CSVs retain image coordinates. In the native 1534 × 899 figure extracted from the supplement PDF, x = 349.5 and 1100.5 pixels correspond to 0 and 5 h. Rows y = 262 and 72 correspond to 100 and 1000 nm. Only points within the coloured field are sampled. The colour-bar ends at y = 395 and 72 span 0–40 000 particles cm⁻³ per decade. The figure labels dN/dlogDp and omits a concentration unit on the colour bar. We interpret it using its decimal-log diameter axis and the experiment’s particle-count convention.

- **run24_psd.csv** contains 273 cells covering 25.12–501.19 nm, sampled every quarter hour from 0 to 5 h. Each bin spans 0.1 in log₁₀(D/nm). Multiply each bin-mean dN/dlog₁₀D by 0.1 to obtain bin counts per cm³. Image pixels map to the nearest colour along the bar, rounded to its approximately 5000 cm⁻³ contour spacing. Pixels more than 38 RGB units from the scale are discarded, masking black, magenta and red overlays. Medians along the local ±4-pixel time window are averaged across available log-diameter rows. At least 46% of each cell's pixels are usable. Perturbing this half-width to 2/7 pixels and colour threshold to 32/46 changes individual cells by ≤1407 cm⁻³ per decade. The 5000 contour increment is a conservative reading scale; it is separate from instrument uncertainty. Zero-colour tails represent unresolved low concentrations.
- **run24_gmd.csv** follows the solid geometric mean diameter line, using a separate intensity mask within a coarse trajectory corridor to avoid the dashed bounds. A ±2 pixel reading tolerance corresponds to roughly 2.5% in diameter. The recorded mean grows from 91.9 nm at lights-on to 222.5 nm at five hours. These GMD and colour records derive from the same underlying size scans.
- Numerical integration of the coloured field yields approximately 16 041 and 3738 particles cm⁻³ at lights-on and five hours. The corresponding WCPC totals are 23 500 and 4600 cm⁻³. The finite plotted diameter range, colour discretization and differing instruments affect this comparison. The geometric mean computed from quantized bin heights also differs from the instrument’s geometric mean line. Use the colour reading scale when fitting absolute cell concentrations.

To regenerate both CSVs, download the linked PDF (SHA-256 `ccc695cd550e3773a95aabcbc45280c2614ee8512542225a8211ec13c3f77a86`), run `pdfimages -f 5 -l 5 -png supplement.pdf wang`, then `python extract_wang_s1.py wang-000.png`. The script verifies the decoded pixel checksum. Derived original graphic © Wang et al. (2022), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Archived DMPS size summaries

Seven distinct run records give a modal particle size labeled `mode` or `mode_onl`. Their DMPS headers leave the mode unit blank. The numeric values lie on successive nominal mobility channels in the range reported for the Manchester DMPS. We interpret the values as nm and retain the original precision. In the 0–5 h study window there are 212 positive mode measurements, spaced about ten minutes apart. Modal size is an individual property of the number distribution and differs from geometric mean. A modeled mode should be taken from dN/dlog₁₀D over the instrument window, with bin choice reported. Run 18 also includes 31 post-light measurements of total surface concentration explicitly labeled nm² cm⁻³. The files include available pre-light samples back to −900 s. Each row in edf_sources.csv links the source DOI, archive URL and original SHA-256. `python extract_edf_sizes.py` regenerates both datasets from those URLs; `--raw /path/to/primary /path/to/other` accepts locally downloaded EDF files.

The campaign overview lists the DMPS range as 40–600 nm and an SMPS as 10–1000 nm. The volatility companion gives 10–670 nm for its SMPS. The campaign-wide seed mode and total area reported in the volatility companion are 67 ± 6 nm and (1.2 ± 0.4) × 10⁹ nm² cm⁻³. They characterize multiple experiments, while the records here identify individual runs. The separate dark-chamber calibration in Shao et al. used seeds with a mode near 100 nm.
