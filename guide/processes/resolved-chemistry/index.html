      React gas and particle materials in a resolved box

      The glyoxal chamber example uses reversible condensation and particle chemistry to compare organic loss and imidazole formation during a measured chamber dilution.

      Use a version 2 mechanism to transform material inside resolved particles or to consume a gas through irreversible reactive uptake. Every particle in a box draws from its own box’s shared gas. Homogeneous gas reactions, bulk material reactions and uptake channels can run in the same coupled solve. The worked uptake and aging example starts from a complete aerosol and compares its history with a radius model.

      Describe the reactions and bind existing lanes

      Section titled “Describe the reactions and bind existing lanes”

      This mechanism deposits gas X as fresh material with a net collision probability of 0.04. Fresh material becomes aged material at 0.35 s⁻¹. The three reservoirs have the same molar mass. The synthetic element X makes the elemental budget explicit.

      Save the mechanism as aging.yaml. Supply the actual material columns when binding. Here column 0 holds an inert core. Gas names match exactly by default. An optional gas_overrides mapping connects a mechanism gas ID to a different simulation name.

      All particle species require explicit molar masses (kg/mol). The binding checks mass balance and checks elemental balance where all participating compositions are supplied. Gas order, molar masses, material mappings and densities belong to the binding identity. Rebind and prepare again when reordering or relabeling material columns. Material storage is positional. Relabeling equal-density columns is observable only through the mapping supplied by the caller. New product columns must already exist, and may start at zero mass. Resolved particle counts, charge and inactive slots retain their values.

      Concentrations and uptake

      Section titled “Concentrations and uptake”

      For a bulk reaction, the instantaneous material volume is the sum of over all constituents of each particle. At each trial stage the concentration of species is in mol/m³ of material. The product of the molar rate density and is the reaction extent in mol/s per physical particle. Use the same constant or Arrhenius fields and per-order rate units as in the gas mechanism guide. Homogeneous gas reactions use mol/m³ of air.

      For reactive uptake the molar extent per particle is , with . The spherical radius follows the current material volume. Each channel consumes one incoming gas molecule and adds its declared material products. The sum of channel probabilities for a gas may be at most one. Each box debits gas by the sum of the same extents multiplied by represented counts and divided by box volume. Gas-kinetic uptake definition

      Prescribe temperature and a net probability appropriate to the application. This law assumes well-mixed gas, negligible gas diffusion resistance and irreversible accommodation and reaction. Explicit adsorption sites, thermal feedback, charge-changing reactions and particle retirement require additional models. Condensation can run in the same sequence. Map the condensable vapor and the reactive gas to their intended physical pathways. In the qualification case, condensation grows the particle with one vapor before a different gas reacts on the increased surface area.

      Run, record and resume

      Section titled “Run, record and resume”

      RK23 advances a common trial state for gas and every active particle in each box. Gas absolute tolerance is mol/m³ of air. For a particle it is mol/m³ of that particle’s initial volume in the current process interval. That fixed numerical scale preserves meaning near a zero product inventory. Physical volume and reaction rates are recomputed at every stage.

      Use CPUSimulation for NumPy recording and checkpointing. Register the resolved adapter to prepare CUDA. A prepared owner retains rates, trial states and the shared inventory on its device.

      CUDA capture is optional. Each replay can take a different number of adaptive trials inside the fixed launches. The checkpoint includes per-box accepted and rejected trial counts under work_counts. The default 256 MiB scratch limit covers six trial vectors, two slot arrays and rates per box. A budget error during preparation reports the required bytes. A failed interval signals device code 110, and an invalid initial geometry or rate signals 111. Concentrations and masses publish together only after all boxes finish. Qualification, costs and limitations
