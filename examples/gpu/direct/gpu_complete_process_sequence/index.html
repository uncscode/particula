    Five direct CUDA process boundaries

    Section titled “Five direct CUDA process boundaries”

    Upload particles, gas and environment once, retain explicit sidecars, and restore the complete CPU result after the ordered calls. Run this script with --cuda to reproduce the published figures. The unflagged command retains its portable Warp CPU regression.

    Observe the complete direct-call sequence

    Section titled “Observe the complete direct-call sequence”

    The CPU snapshots below show the combined result of condensation, coagulation, dilution and wall loss. The nucleation call has zero duration: it demonstrates its boundary without injecting particles. The prepared lifecycle study adds an active source pulse and a per-process inventory ledger. These before/after panels do not attribute changes to individual processes or claim closed-system conservation in the presence of sinks.
