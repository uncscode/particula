# University and laboratory sources for published aerosol and cloud data

Use this catalog to find a paper, its data, and a specific quantity to compare. On November 20, 2026, the CERN CLOUD CSV and a sample of the Melpitz activation table were read directly. The other entries draw on repository records, instrument listings and publications. For a selected study, check the files and their units before fitting a model or reporting a result.

## US chamber experiments

UC Davis's Integrated Chamber Atmospheric data Repository for Unified Science (ICARUS) is a common entry point for US chamber groups. Its experiment records can include instrument files, injection timelines, chamber conditions and associated papers. Search by reactant, oxidant, seed, instrument or laboratory. The ICARUS access statement specifies free registration for downloads and describes PI notification and citation for publication. Individual NCAR Geoscience Data Exchange (GDEX) records also list their own licenses. [Experiment search](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/search) · [Contributing groups](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/about) · [ICARUS access and use statement](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/organization/540)

| Laboratory | Experiment and observable | Data and publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carnegie Mellon University, Center for Atmospheric Particle Studies | **Sulfuric-acid nucleation.** The Jen lab measured particle formation in a flow tube with individual amines and mixtures. Johnson and Jen explicitly identify experiment 754 in their paper. Its record lists the compiled CSV and an injection timeline. A separate CAPS series tracks organic exchange between model particle populations. | [Nucleation data](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experiment/754) · [Johnson and Jen, 2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-22-8287-2022) · [Particle mixing series](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experimentset/222) |
| California Institute of Technology, Seinfeld chambers | **α-Pinene photooxidation.** FIXCIT has separate low- and high-NO runs with hydroxyl radicals under dry conditions. In the low-NO run, ammonium sulfate seed was added after about five hours, after the lights were turned off. That timing matters when interpreting the organic aerosol mass response. | [Low-NO run](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d789020/) · [High-NO run](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d789022/) · [Nguyen et al., campaign overview](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-14-13531-2014) |
| Georgia Institute of Technology, Ng group | **Mixed-precursor secondary organic aerosol.** Compare simultaneous and sequential nitrate-radical oxidation of α-pinene and limonene on ammonium sulfate seed. The sequential record lists particle-volume time series, aerosol mass spectra, gas measurements and the injection sequence. | [Simultaneous run](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d789574/) · [Sequential run and files](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experiment/855) · [Mixtures paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35546-1) |
| University of California, Davis, Nguyen group | **Size-dependent particle wall loss.** Ammonium sulfate run 925 in the 10 m³ chamber lists size distributions over time and a separate file of loss coefficients β (s⁻¹) by particle diameter (nm). Compare predicted decay against the distributions and the authors’ derived rates. The file preview requested a verified email. | [Experiment series](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experimentset/265) · [Run 925 and column names](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experiment/925) · [Wall-loss paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/02786826.2023.2298219) |
| University of Colorado Boulder, Jimenez lab | **Gas–particle–wall partitioning.** Ammonium sulfate run 237 lists gas-phase nitrate-CIMS signals, the particle condensation sink (s⁻¹), integrated number concentration (cm⁻³) and chamber conditions. CIMS signals need the paper’s interpretation before use as vapor concentrations. | [Run 237 and column names](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experiment/237) · [Liu et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-019-0200-x) |

| University of California, Irvine, Nizkorodov lab | **Toluene aerosol formation and subsequent aging.** Run 772 lists chamber ozone, nitrogen oxides and aerosol mass files. For a bleaching comparison, use the paper and supplement to identify the optical measurements for each matrix. | [Run 772](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experiment/772) · [Chamber series](https://icarus.ucdavis.edu/experimentset/247) · [Klodt et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-22-10155-2022) |

## Wildfire smoke from the laboratory to the atmosphere

| Institution and setting | What the data resolve | Data and publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDA Forest Service, Missoula Fire Sciences Laboratory, Montana · controlled burns | **FIREX FireLab 2016.** NOAA archives fuel and fire metadata alongside instrument-specific records for smoke emissions and processing. Use the fire number and sampling period to match a burn to particle size, number or fine-mode composition. Lim and colleagues used this campaign to study secondary organic aerosol formation during smoke oxidation. The download page asks users to acknowledge the campaign data policy. | [Data download](https://csl.noaa.gov/groups/csl7/measurements/2016firex/FireLab/DataDownload/) · [Instrument IDs](https://csl.noaa.gov/groups/csl7/measurements/2016firex/FireLab/dataidtable.html) · [Lim et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-19-12797-2019) |
| Desert Research Institute, Reno · ground-based observations | **Wildfire-influenced ambient size distributions.** Lu and colleagues compared smoke-influenced and background days in Reno. The Zenodo deposit lists one hourly HDF5 file of scanning mobility particle sizer observations from 2017, 2018 and 2020. Use the paper's smoke-day classification when selecting periods. | [Version 1 dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/14740745) · [Lu et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5EA00007F) |
| Colorado State University · aircraft observations | **WE-CAN 2018.** CSU measured fine-mode aerosol composition aboard the NSF/NCAR C-130 from July 24 through September 13, 2018. NCAR specifies ICARTT files with units and quality notes in the headers. For a brown-carbon comparison, match flight and sampling interval across the composition and optical instruments. The paper lists separate DOIs for each. | [CSU aircraft AMS data](https://doi.org/10.26023/MM2Y-ZGFQ-RB0B) · [Extinction, scattering and absorption](https://doi.org/10.26023/K8P0-X4T3-TN06) · [Paper and companion datasets](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/24/12881/2024/acp-24-12881-2024-assets.html) |

An earlier Missoula campaign, FLAME-4 (2012), provides a complementary fuel-by-fuel source for trace-gas emission factors measured by infrared spectroscopy. The paper identifies the fuels, sampling configuration and measurements for interpreting the published tables. [Stockwell et al., 2014, with supplement](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/14/9727/2014/index.html)

## Other laboratory and field datasets

- **CERN CLOUD chamber, nucleation rates.** The archived CSV matched the repository checksum and contained 377 rows with six columns. It pairs temperature (K), sulfuric acid (10⁶ cm⁻³), ammonia (ppt), ionization rate (cm⁻³ s⁻¹) and relative humidity (%) with nucleation rate (cm⁻³ s⁻¹). Some ammonia values were inferred. Use the paper’s supplement when selecting conditions for a rate comparison. [Data S1 and units](https://zenodo.org/records/13981565) · [Dunne et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf2649)
- **TROPOS, Melpitz research station, cloud condensation nuclei.** The 2012–2016 bundle has separate activation, size-distribution and composition records. The activation table has UTC time, supersaturation (%), fitted critical dry diameter (nm) and two derived CCN concentrations (cm⁻³). One uses the activation-fraction curve and the other uses the critical diameter. Choose the definition that matches the model calculation. [Activation table](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.938213) · [Mobility size distributions](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.938214) · [Composition and absorption instruments](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.938211) · [Wang et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-22-15943-2022)

## Finding datasets across universities

- **Search by paper or author.** DataCite Commons can surface related datasets when the DOI metadata links the paper and data. If the relationship is missing, try the paper title and check its data availability statement. [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/) · [Search and related works](https://support.datacite.org/docs/works-in-datacite-commons)
- **Search by chamber process.** The ACTRIS Atmospheric Simulation Chamber portal collects experiments from multiple facilities and indexes derived products such as secondary organic aerosol yields and vapor pressures. [Chamber data portal](https://data.eurochamp.org/)
- **Search by field observable.** PANGAEA hosts the EUSAAR size-distribution collection, which brings together harmonized dry particle observations from 24 European sites over two years. [EUSAAR dataset](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.861856) · [Asmi et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-11-5505-2011)
