      particula.execution.multivapor_parcel

      CUDA conditional-graph solver for the finite multivapor parcel.

      The resident coordinates are pressure, temperature and analytical material masses. Each RHS reconstructs its finite gas inventories by conservation. This formulation and embedded solvers are independent of the Radau oracle, which integrates the gas reservoirs explicitly.
