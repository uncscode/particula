      class EffectiveIndexModel(grid, *, relative_humidity=None, sizing_relative_humidity=None, kappa=None, growth_factor=None, kelvin_length=1.05e-09, water_index=1.333, device='numpy')

      Bases: _OpticalModel

      Fit a common complex index, with optional water uptake.

      With wet sizes use the default configuration for an index at the optical RH. With dry sizes supply relative_humidity and either a fixed kappa or leave kappa open for fitting. Alternatively supply a measured diameter growth factor to determine water volume directly. Supply sizing RH if the measured distribution retains water at a known equilibrium RH. This model shares one dry index across all wavelengths and RH groups.

      Prescribed composition with internal/external number fractions by size.

      Each dry bin has a monodisperse internal population and size-matched pure-species external populations. The endpoints have the same dry number density and per-bin species inventories. Volume fractions may be uniform across bins or specified per group and bin.

      class OpticalGrid(radius: np.ndarray, number_density: np.ndarray, wavelength: np.ndarray)

      One particle spectrum per optical wavelength and measurement window.

      Radius and number_density have shape (group, bin). A one-dimensional spectrum broadcasts over groups. Radii and vacuum wavelengths are in metres and number densities are integrated counts per bin in m⁻³. Groups can repeat a wavelength at different humidity or time conditions.

      class OpticalObservations(response, values, *, standard_deviation=None, covariance=None)

      Calibrated measurements projected from (group, E/S/A) coefficients.

      Response has shape (observation, group, 3). Use it to select a channel or average predicted spectra across measured time or spectral windows. Use only measured channels with an independent uncertainty model. Observations and standard deviations have units m⁻¹. Covariance entries have units m⁻². Include environmental and sizing uncertainty through sensitivity cases or a supplied covariance.

      def fit_optical(model, observations, *, bounds, starts, method='autodiff', max_nfev=None)

      Fit optically constrained parameters from one or more bounded starts.

      Sensitivity is the whitened Jacobian per parameter in its native units. Singular values include structural zeros when there are fewer independent observations than unknowns. Inspect profiles and held-out predictions before treating a least-squares solution as a unique retrieval.

      profile_opticalfunction#

      View source
      def profile_optical(model, observations, fit, parameter, coordinates, *, bounds, method='autodiff', delta_chi_square=3.841459)

      Profile a fitted parameter by optimizing all others at fixed values.

      Returns the grid, profiled chi square, and the intervals resolved by the grid and threshold. A component touching either search bound is limited by that bound. The default delta is the asymptotic 95% cutoff for one parameter under a regular Gaussian measurement model.
