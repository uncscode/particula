      OpticalHeatingclass#

      View source
      class OpticalHeating(optics, irradiance, *, gas_water, water_material=0, physics=None, name='optical_heating')

      Bases: BoxProcess

      Heat a particle-resolved water/solute population with irradiance.

      The irradiances are band-integrated W/m², one value per configured wavelength. Absorption is recomputed from live wet sizes and composition at the start of the process. Use attach_fields() before preparing the sequence. Each box exchanges heat with its fixed-temperature gas and a grey radiation bath. Evaporated water enters the named gas species.

      The liquid, spherical continuum model neglects curvature in the vapor balance. Its transport coefficients are prescribed. Complete optical heat exchange and mass loss are implicit in particle temperature. Geometry, irradiance, heat capacity and surrounding gas are evaluated at the beginning of each cycle.
