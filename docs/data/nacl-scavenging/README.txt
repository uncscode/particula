# NaCl scavenging data

Runlong Cai deposited the figure data accompanying Cai et al. (2022) under CC BY 4.0. `source-data.zip` is the original unmodified archive (SHA-256 `99af0abd636ae4a4031d99cd922619796f24c1ba164ae1d69babee2128663593`). Measurements and published theory columns in the derived tables use those data. [Original dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6982767) · [Article](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-22-11529-2022) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The remaining files record Particula calculations. `coefficients.csv` gives the neutral benchmarks and `survival.csv` the growing-tracer calculation. `charge-fractions.csv` and `charge-scenarios.csv` give the monodisperse charge study. `wall-fits.csv` contains six blank decay fits. `steady.csv` contains the continuous-injection parameter sweep, `gpu-summary.csv` the four-seed pulse ensembles, and `comparison.csv` the five measured sizes with the steady retrievals. `run.json` identifies the original GPU, source revision, and run count.

archived-4nm-scan.csv gives the modeled steady response at the seven reported channel centers and the reported flow settings. The source is Runlong Cai’s updated dataset (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13291658), member metadata/timeseries/SMPSchamber20210729_4nm.txt of metadata.zip. Its data.zip matches the earlier figure archive byte for byte.

capture-steady.csv reports 168 continuous-source retrievals across five diameters and nine case names. Model names dispersion_only and polarization_only toggle the two material forces while retaining the signed bare Coulomb interaction. All four main controls use the same earlier hard-sphere rate for pairs outside the capture domain. full_zero_tail and full_legacy_tail vary that assumption. excluded_pairs is the out-of-domain fraction weighted by steady tracer and collector counts, while excluded_size and excluded_unlike can overlap. For full rows, the four full_*_cm3_s columns partition the supported true capture flux per pair by charge class. tail_bare_cm3_s completes the total. The subsets tracer_neutral and collector_neutral each require a charged partner. capture-pairs.csv contains public CPU pair rates in cm³/s for dry NaCl at the nominal diameters. capture-run.json records the CUDA campaign, material, revision and environment.

other-kernels-pairs.csv gives nominal geometric pair rates in cm³/s. other-kernels-steady.csv gives 209 steady retrievals and their differences from the full dispersion and dielectric reference. The full_* rows add mechanical rates to that reference. Other rows replace its thermal law. The _neutral suffix sets the collision-law charges to zero while retaining the same instrument and ion physics. AO2008 is a diagnostic extrapolation from cloud droplets. other-kernels-run.json identifies the revision, device and assumed air-flow parameters.
