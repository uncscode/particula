# Liu terpene evaporation data

The six CSV files in [digitized.zip](digitized.zip) preserve readings from Liu et al. and their associated coordinates. The paper and supplement can be retrieved from the authors. Figure 4 and the supporting information are credited to Sijia Liu and coauthors. The particle volumes are apparent dry volumes inferred from changes in peak vacuum aerodynamic diameter. [Paper and reuse terms](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsestair.5c00226) · [Supporting information](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/60675517)

| Archive member | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| sources/observations.csv | Figure 4 marker centers in native pixels, elapsed time in minutes, fraction remaining, starting mobility diameter in nm and density in g cm⁻³ |
| sources/fractions.csv | Seven volatility-bin fractions transcribed from Tables S4 and S5, with saturation concentrations in µg m⁻³ |
| sources/time_verification.csv | Figure 4 marker times matched to printed diameter-spectrum times |
| sources/peak_check.csv | Independent peak-center readings for the initial and late spectra |
| uncertainty/peaks.csv | Native Figure S4 pixel coordinates of initial, later and adjacent diameter samples |
| viscosity/figure7/figure7_viscosity.csv | Formula-based viscosity readings at selected humidities, with axis calibration and ceiling status |

[figure2.csv](figure2.csv) contains the 20-hour forecast envelopes pooled across five chamber configurations. The half-bin rows cover four precursors, two mechanisms and six diameters. Two full-bin rows provide the sensitivity check for 60 nm ocimene. Low and high are fractions of initial dry volume. The accepted count sums eligible model parameter sets across configurations.
