# Observations behind the sea-spray sulfate study

The observation archive preserves the readings a researcher would otherwise have to make again from published figures. It contains 14 CSVs, including pixel coordinates where recorded, the Rosati number spectra, and alternate integrations of the six chromatograms. Six further CSVs come from the authors’ tables, workbook cells and printed results. The preparation command reconstructs these and checks all 20 against their SHA-256 fingerprints. [Graphical observations](observations.zip) · [Preparation code and source manifests](../../../studies/xiong-sea-spray/data/) · [Scientific replay](../../../studies/xiong-sea-spray/README.md)

## Reproduce the inputs

From the repository root, use Python 3.12 or later and Poppler's pdftotext. The first run downloads the publisher sources and checks their SHA-256 hashes. Subsequent runs reuse the checked cache. Replace the example directories with locations of your choice.

    python studies/xiong-sea-spray/data/prepare.py --output /tmp/xiong-inputs --cache /tmp/xiong-publisher-cache

To reread the figures from their original rasters, add --verify-sources. This also needs Poppler's pdfimages, NumPy 2 or later and Pillow. It reproduces the CSVs and writes six visual checks beside them for Xiong Figures 5, 6, S19 and S20, Rosati Figure 2b, and the earlier phase diagram. Use --digitizations-only to extract the 14 graphical CSVs offline with Python's standard library. Pass the full prepared directory as --inputs to the scientific replay. [Source URLs and SHA-256](../../../studies/xiong-sea-spray/data/publishers.json) · [Expected observation checksums](../../../studies/xiong-sea-spray/data/observations.json)

## Graphical observations

Xijie Xiong et al. aged deposited size-selected particles at 85% RH, dried them, and measured the optical growth during rehumidification. Figures 4 and 5 use a wet reference at 80% RH. Their sulfate and remaining-chloride estimates share growth measurements and assumptions. A graphical reading allowance describes uncertainty in extracting the figure. Where the authors plot experimental bars, the CSVs preserve them separately. [Xiong et al., main article and figures](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-26-12183-2026) · [Supplementary figures and tables](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/26/12183/2026/acp-26-12183-2026-supplement.pdf)

Rosati et al. measured artificial sea-salt aerosol in a separate chamber. Only the three measured number distributions transfer into the NaCl aging calculation. The two diameters compared in the study, 200 and 300 nm, both fall in the mobility-instrument range. ## Recovering the author tables

- Xijie Xiong et al. Table S1 supplies all 294 post-exposure growth factors and printed spreads for seven diameters, six readout humidities and seven aging times (xiong/table-s1.csv). Table S2 supplies 108 optical readings for six organic volume fractions at six humidities and three aging times (aiomfac/coating/figure6-s2.csv). Section 3.1.1 supplies ten whole-hour relative changes at 100 nm (xiong/text-100nm.csv). Their methods describe an SOS-excluded growth calculation. This complicates a direct interpretation of coated-particle growth as a whole-sphere diameter. [Main paper](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/26/12183/2026/acp-26-12183-2026.pdf) · [Supplement](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/26/12183/2026/acp-26-12183-2026-supplement.pdf)
- Chun Xiong et al. measured the onset of visible liquid separation while drying droplets containing sodium octyl sulfate (C₈H₁₇OSO₃Na) with NaCl or ammonium sulfate. The workbook exchanges the octyl sulfate and octane sulfonate labels in its “Figure 6” sheet. The orange sulfate series in the published graph and the numerical text in §3.2.1 identify rows 4 and 14 as sulfate. The resulting parameter-fit/phase-data.csv keeps the original labels and cell addresses, SRH means and standard deviations, and companion deliquescence and efflorescence values. Its onset temperatures are inferred from 610 Pa water vapor pressure. At 90% organic volume with NaCl, the sulfate series has no visible liquid separation before efflorescence. The 56.4% RH observation at that fraction belongs to the sulfonate. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-23-8979-2023) · [Published Figure 6](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/23/8979/2023/acp-23-8979-2023-f06-web.png) · [Zenodo workbook](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8079001)
- Shahabadi et al. provide four bulk aqueous sodium octyl sulfate measurements in the “Fig. 2” sheet, cells B19:D22, at 295 K. The density and molarity give the mass of water per liter as ρ − cM, with ρ in kg L⁻¹ and surfactant molar mass M = 0.23227 kg mol⁻¹. Meter accuracy is ±0.003 in water activity. Peng et al. Table 1 cell C23 gives 7.63 ± 0.02 mol water per mol surfactant at 90% RH and 298.15 K. These five observations make up parameter-fit/binary-water.csv. Peng Table 2 cells A3:A14 and D3:D14 supply the separate particle growth test at 100 nm dry mobility diameter and 297.15 K in parameter-fit/binary-aerosol-check.csv. Both parameter-fit paths are under aiomfac/coating/. [Shahabadi supplement](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/25/14301/2025/acp-25-14301-2025-supplement.zip) · [Peng Table 1](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/21/7135/2021/acp-21-7135-2021-t01.xlsx) · [Peng Table 2](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/21/7135/2021/acp-21-7135-2021-t02.xlsx)
