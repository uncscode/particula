# Particle processes

<a id="processes-connect-a-mechanism-to-an-observable-change"></a>

Choose the change you need to explain, then follow its number and material
budgets. Condensation transfers material between phases; coagulation merges
particles; nucleation and injection create a population through different
sources; dilution and walls remove it. Similar-looking spectra can arise from
different combinations.

Start with one mechanism and a quantity it must conserve or transfer. Condensation exchanges material between particles and gas, coagulation changes number through mergers, and dilution or wall deposition removes particles. Check the [initial rate and reservoir tutorials](../../tutorials/condensation/index.md) before coupling growth to a source or loss.
