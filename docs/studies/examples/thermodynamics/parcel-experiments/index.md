# Parcel replay and exchange

Replay a recorded warm-cloud ascent to measure how sampling affects droplet growth.
Then follow organic vapor and individual settling particles across shared parcel faces.
For larger layouts, compare lofting in a vertical stack, a periodic horizontal row,
and a grid traveling with the air. The ascent replay and two-cell exchange studies also measure sensitivities on CUDA.
