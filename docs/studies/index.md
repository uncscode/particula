Find a calculation by its scientific question, learn a workflow, or inspect the observations and reference solutions behind a result.

If you are starting with a measured population, begin with [the first CPU simulation](../guide/getting-started/first-simulation.md). To investigate a process, choose a diagnostic you can check independently, such as species mass, number density, or growth rate. A conserved total can coexist with an inaccurate trajectory. Use the [topic explorer](../guide/topics/index.md) to trace that question through the scientific background.
