# Droplet measurements, figure values and replay

Sagan and colleagues published the chamber measurement as time–diameter panels and a pooled spectrum. The numbers below are our transcriptions of those panels and the measured gas histories used in the calculation. The article and supplemental figures remain available from their original hosts. Raw holograms, individual droplet tracks and run-specific camera calibrations were unavailable for this comparison. [Sagan et al., article](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0344133) · [NOAA article PDF](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/76087/noaa_76087_DS1.pdf) · [Supplementary material](https://doi.org/10.60893/figshare.jcp.32982017)

- [Digitized observations (ZIP)](../../../../website/public/study-data/reach-droplets/digitizations.zip) contains the occupied Figure 7 cells, plotted times including empty columns, separately drawn Figure 7c curves, Figure 6 gas and droplet traces and the five radial temperatures from supplemental Figure S2. [Publisher’s supplement and terms](https://aip.figshare.com/articles/figure/Supplementary_Material/32982017)
- [Values behind every figure (ZIP)](../../../../website/public/study-data/reach-droplets/figure-data.zip) contains CSV tables and a figure-by-figure key. It includes the quarter-second spectra that produce both density and difference maps, the separately digitized pooled observation evaluated at the plotted bin centers, the within-record uncertainty bounds, the four-family fits and both cameras’ change in density and concentration. The placement table includes all six displacements, both normalizations and the beam offsets. Reuse the supplied observations, fitted curves and predicted second-camera series under their respective labels.

## How to read the optical reconstruction

Figure 7a,b displays occupied cells on a time and diameter grid. We located that grid from the PDF vector coordinates and decoded cell colors relative to the color scale. Each published concentration already includes a size-dependent optical-volume correction. Some colors saturate. The extraction preserves the clipped-cell flag and uses the size-specific count quantum and neighboring occupancy to estimate compatible counts. The scored distribution covers the 68 interior bins from 11 to 79 µm. Time is seconds from recording start, with the expansion valve triggered at 0.5 s. Figure 7c independently renders the mean of the same holograms over 4–7 s. It checks the extraction over that window. [Measurement limits and uncertainty](camera.md)

Figure 6 temperatures and vapor traces were aligned to the published time axis, read within each trace segment and averaged into 50 ms intervals. The displayed 0.004 offset on the fan-off vapor trace was removed. For Figure S2 we transcribed the five radial temperature series near the upper boundary. Pressure after expansion and the assumed spatial interpolation enter the model separately. The original apparatus illustration and window drawing constrain possible beam positions. [REACh apparatus paper](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0255658) · [Engineering drawing](https://doi.org/10.60893/figshare.rsi.28843976.v1)

## Repeat the comparison

The downloadable worked example integrates the 50–65 µm concentration during the same half-second for the measurement, forward simulation and fitted spatial update. The study source includes the digitizers, figure renderers, individual-droplet and sectional models, automatic-differentiation fit, calibrated camera interventions and commands for scientific replay. Plotting reads the archived figure values and runs on a CPU. The scientific replay requires NVIDIA CUDA; the original calculations used an RTX 5060 and Particula revision `bf91687e521d24689486e721d6073250e6ce85f6`.
