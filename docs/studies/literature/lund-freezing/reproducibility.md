# Reproduce the Lund freezing and entrainment comparisons

The author observations are event times in repeated freeze–thaw assays. The cloud profiles come from published intercomparison specifications. The ice, particle tracks and water fields in the cloud figures are outputs of the prescribed-flow simulations. To inspect the main result, start with the three-row table. Each row gives the mean six-hour event concentrations for fresh inflow and both controls, followed by the paired differences and their 95% intervals in events per litre of cloud air. [Download the baseline table](https://github.com/uncscode/particula/blob/main/docs/data/lund-freezing/entrainment-baselines.csv) · [Return to the study](index.md)

| Result | Observations or model | Files used to reproduce it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figure 1 and the short-hold supplement | Jakobsson et al.’s raw events and Tables 3 and 4, short-hold fits and overnight forecasts | `fetch_inputs.py` retrieves and verifies the four inputs. `study.py` reads the wells, fits the rates and scores the overnight records. `figures.py` draws the reference curves. |
| Synthetic two-hour exchange | Conditional activity of wells still liquid at hold entry or at two hours | `challenges.py`, `landmark.py`, `uncertainty.py`, `duration.py` and `refinement.py` test the fits. `boxes.py` runs the CUDA pulse experiments. |
| Reference-eddy histories, tagged tracks and Animation 1 | Water transport on the Particula grid and particle histories in a prescribed circulation | `cloud-eddy/cloud.py` configures the flow. `physics.py` advances particles and phase. `analyze.py` compares outcomes and draws the static figures. `animate_eddy.py` renders the movie from the recorded track run. |
| Figure 2, the graphical abstract and alternative aerosol sources | Matched seeds for fresh and survivor-like inflows or a closed top | `cloud-sources/campaign.py` sets up the comparisons. `loading.py` translates Table 1 into well equivalents per litre. `summarize.py` pairs runs and constructs intervals. `replot.py` draws Figure 2 and calls the source and cloud figure routines. |
| Figure 3 and the mineral and moisture comparisons | Prescribed ISDAC and M-PACE profiles, source-dependent freezing, one-way exchange and water supply | `isdac.py` and `mpace.py` initialize the profiles. `minerals.py` implements the active-site fits. `unsteady.py` supplies the variable circulation. `plots.py` renders the moisture and mineral panels. |

The numerical archive contains the plotted values, the sample-averaged forecast curves, tagged paths used in the static panel, and the completed run configurations. The source guide gives commands for redrawing all eleven static figures from those values, checking the author files, and replaying selected or full CUDA calculations. Each measurement can be retrieved from its author or journal. The same procedure reconstructs the simulation histories and movie.
