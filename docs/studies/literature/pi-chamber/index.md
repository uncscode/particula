# Π Chamber experiments and modeling

The Π Chamber experiments counted both haze and cloud droplets over fresh-water and salt-water floors. The haze and cloud study uses haze to fit interior humidity and tests the prediction against the measured cloud counts. The companion asks which seed particles the chamber optics could detect if the seed chemistry changed.

For single-particle water uptake and activation thresholds under prescribed conditions, see [Particle water uptake and activation](../particle-water-uptake/index.md).
