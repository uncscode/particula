# Evaporation and reactive uptake

These studies follow organic material as it moves between gas and condensed phases. They compare evaporation from a fed pentane drop, glyoxal uptake and release in wet aerosol, and the size and organic mass of aerosol formed from precursor mixtures in the Manchester chamber.
