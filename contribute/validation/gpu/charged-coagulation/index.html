      Charged coagulation and atmospheric ion validation

      Current corrected-model qualification

      Section titled “Current corrected-model qualification”

      The corrected CPU/GPU charged Knudsen normalization passed a fresh twenty-case CUDA qualification on 2026-11-14. The frozen specification records revision 54ad1a812, 8192 independent boxes per case and root seed 320. Its helper hash still matches the maintained source. All sixteen individual conservative 95% probability-difference intervals lie inside the predeclared [-0.01, 0.01] tolerance; the largest absolute endpoint is 0.00714698. All four baseline interval widths satisfy the separate 0.01 sampling-precision target. These are individual comparison coverages, not a simultaneous 95% statement.

      Ion source [pairs cm^-3 s^-1] Omitted coefficient assumption Reached Captured Unresolved Baseline interval width
      2 0 m³/s 142 8050 0 0.00577974
      2 1e-15 m³/s 142 8050 0 0.00577974
      10 0 m³/s 154 8038 0 0.00600904
      10 1e-15 m³/s 154 8038 0 0.00600904

      Every case resolved all original lineages. The largest per-species mass residual over the campaign was 3.109e-15, and every box conserved exact net charge. Each doubled-capacity case preserved the entire recorded time/outcome series exactly, checked independently after execution. Maximum observed occupancy was 37 slots; diagnostic samples do not bound occupancy between observations. The complete specification, twenty comparisons, case summaries and capacity checks are saved in docs/data/gpu/charged-survival-corrected-qualification.json.

      The current package regression and environmental qualification are recorded in environmental validation. Concurrent CUDA jobs mean campaign timings are not standalone performance measurements.

      Completed corrected-model publication

      Section titled “Completed corrected-model publication”

      The five-case CUDA publication used the separate root seed 321 and 8192 boxes per case. Its final generation is run-20261114T203855083494Z, with revision 799e75d92 and source/helper hashes in the manifest. The previous generation was removed after this successful refresh; only the final output remains.

      Ion source [pairs cm^-3 s^-1] Omitted coefficient assumption Reached Captured Success fraction Exact 95% interval
      0 Strict lookup, no ions 163 8029 0.01990 [0.01698, 0.02316]
      2 0 m³/s 152 8040 0.01855 [0.01574, 0.02172]
      2 1e-15 m³/s 152 8040 0.01855 [0.01574, 0.02172]
      10 0 m³/s 170 8022 0.02075 [0.01778, 0.02408]
      10 1e-15 m³/s 170 8022 0.02075 [0.01778, 0.02408]

      All lineages resolved by 3730–3750 s. About 98% were captured before reaching 50 nm; the cumulative-success curves rise only near the growth time of the surviving minority. The q=2 point estimate is below the no-ion control and the q=10 estimate is above it. However, the individual conservative 95% ion-minus-control intervals are [-0.00827, 0.00559] for q=2 and [-0.00626, 0.00797] for q=10 under either omission assumption. Each includes zero, so this realization does not resolve an ion effect. Matching outcomes under both coefficient assumptions do not establish identical probabilities or turn those assumptions into guaranteed survival bounds. The one-percentage-point numerical criterion does not establish smaller effects.

      Every box conserved exact net charge; maximum species-mass residuals were about 2.89e-15. Observed peak occupancy was 33, 35 and 37 slots for q=0, 2 and 10, respectively, against capacity 128. The final three-panel figure was visually inspected; source/helper/figure hashes match, with one generation retained. The complete refresh took 2030.59 s while the humidity study also ran on the GPU; this is execution provenance, not a throughput comparison.

      The independently extracted download passed all five small CUDA smoke cases with eight boxes and a 100 s horizon; Python source and helper bytes match. That smoke test checks execution and inventory/lineage bookkeeping, not survival precision.

      Implemented boundary

      Section titled “Implemented boundary”

      The five existing charged models can run directly or through prepared Warp execution. A bounded parallel reduction supplies the exact pair maximum to the existing stochastic sampler. Prepared CUDA capture and same-device checkpoint continuation retain the selected model. Existing Brownian defaults remain.

      The CPU Dyachkov dimensionless formula now evaluates differences with expm1 and an equivalent rearrangement to avoid cancellation near neutrality. The analytic neutral limit is checked independently; CUDA agreement does not define that limit. CUDA decimal fit coefficients are retained as explicit fp64 constants rather than rounding through float32 literals.

      IonPairSource injects explicit positive/negative particles with unit counts, independent persistent source randomness, a fixed-capacity rejection boundary, and cumulative external-mass accounting. IonNeutralCleanup exports only neutral particles made entirely from both reserved ion materials, with persistent species mass and particle ledgers. Neither establishes ion collision physics or the completed Lucky Few survival study.

      The LF2013 helper rejects unsupported domains by default. Optional lower/upper values for omitted repulsive fluxes are explicit coefficient assumptions, not bounds on survival probability or an extrapolated fit. Same-sign charged seeds with diameter 3 nm fall below the corresponding fit’s lower radius, approximately 5.308 nm. On 2026-11-14 the user selected both coefficient assumptions for the survival sensitivity demonstration; neither is a fitted best estimate.

      Neutral-product cleanup follow-up

      Section titled “Neutral-product cleanup follow-up”

      Cleanup tests check that neutral aerosols remain, charged ion-only particles remain, each exported species is accounted for, repeated cleanup is a no-op, and ledger/state continuation survives checkpoint and native CUDA capture.
